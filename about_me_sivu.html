Portfolio

Telia Cygate: IT Operations Center (2024 -)

Job description

Work at the ITOC service center is strongly based on the ITILv4 framework, particularly incident management. Other key processes include problem management, change management, and service request fulfillment. I work at the ITOC as a systems specialist, serving clients in the areas of networking, server infrastructure, and information security. My duties involve providing support for information systems, data communications, and security, as well as tasks such as the remote management, configuration, and monitoring of network devices. I handle fault situations within both Telia Cygate’s and clients' environments, aiming to resolve issues in collaboration with the clients' own specialists.>

Work experience

Working in ITOC is varied, as Telia Cygate has a wide range of customers using diverse services; we continuously monitor these and resolve issues in accordance with SLA requirements. Communication with customers takes place primarily via phone, email, or ticketing systems in Finnish and English. On the job, I get to familiarize myself with the network environments of various companies and among other things solve problems related to SD-WAN, LAN, WLAN, firewalls, and servers. Since both our customers and Telia Cygate’s own environments utilize equipment from multiple manufacturers, I gain experience with a wide variety of devices. Troubleshooting is a major part of our daily routine — incident management is, after all, our team’s core function which allows me to deepen my understanding of different technologies, master a range of tools, and learn to read and analyze various types of data, such as network device logs. I also regularly gain experience collaborating with field technicians on installations and hardware replacements.

What have I learned

Working as a Systems Specialist at ITOC is my first role with a strong focus on networking; it has been a valuable learning experience, as the unit serves as a point of contact for many other departments. Through ITOC, I have gained insight into which technologies interest me and which ones I would like to explore further as I build my expertise. For instance, I am particularly interested in fundamental networking skills - an interest that motivated me to earn my Cisco CCNA certification alongside my work. This type of IT role fosters an independent work ethic, requires daily learning, and perhaps most importantly teaches the courage to tackle unfamiliar problems. Strong customer service skills, a suitably relaxed attitude, a sense of humor, and perseverance also go a long way in this job. In the future, I would be interested in deepening my expertise in scripting and automation tools.

Stora Enso: Global Service Desk Nordic (2023)

Job description

I worked as a Service Desk Agent and my job was to solve IT problems that Stora Enso's employees had at their workplace. The users contacted me either from Finland or Sweden by telephone or via email so I had to serve them in Finnish or Swedish as necessary. Their problems mainly considered Stora Enso's internal services, login information, licenses, hardware or network issues, VPN or software problems. These kinds of issues were not always easy to solve on telephone why I used different kinds of remote connectivity softwares. In addition I had to manage user accounts or other data.

Work experience

During the workday, I switched between languages ​​depending on whether the calls I received were from Finland or Sweden. I also participated in meetings conducted in English, as we worked closely with another Global Service Desk unit. I learned troubleshooting techniques, familiarized myself with Windows diagnostic tools, analyzed technical issues, and picked up new technical tricks. Resolving issues also put my ability to handle pressure and my teamwork skills to the test. When necessary, I could ask colleagues for help or send follow-up questions to various teams to find a solution. I achieved the goals I had set for myself: I improved my ability to provide service in Swedish, gained insight into what working at a Service Desk is like, boosted my professional confidence, and familiarized myself with tools, services, and systems that were previously unknown to me.

What did I learn

Working at the Service Desk gave me a glimpse into the vast scope of the IT field and the endless opportunities for learning it offers. Through this work, I developed a growing interest in administrative tasks, the mechanics of network protocols, and hands-on work with network devices. The job also sparked a desire to learn more about cloud services and artificial intelligence; these are technologies of great societal significance that impact our daily lives in unpredictable ways. I believe that in future roles, an entrepreneurial mindset, a curiosity to learn new things, and strong interpersonal skills will be just as essential as technical expertise - since technical skills can certainly be acquired on the job.

My hobbies

Recording

The trombone is not the most popular instrument in the world when we compare for instance the amount of recordings for the trombone and for the piano. The lack of recordings has made me eager to make recordings of my own. It's quite fun to make your own compositions or arrangements and record them on tape. It's also nice to use finished recordings in other projects. On recording sessons I like to use the following, simple setup: Audacity software, AKG D40 dynamic instrument microphone, JBL Tune750BT headphones and Focusrite Scarlett sound card. Playing the Trombone

Making music on spare time is one of the best things I know. One of the greatest things in trombone playing is ensemble playing, especially in a symphony orchestra, in a trombone quartet or in a big band. I mainly play classical music since I've grown up listening to that genre but I would like to learn more jazz or funk trombone techniques also later in life. Like many other trombonists, I prefer the tenor trombone as my first choise of instrument but occationally I like to play the bass trombone in big bands and the alto trombone in symphony orchestras aswell.

Thaiboxing

Thaiboxing, also known as Muay Thai or 'the art of eight limbs', is another important hobby for me. It helps me to forget my worries of everyday life and physically keeps me in shape. Thaiboxing is a very versatile sport where one commonly uses techniques like: boxing (a little bit like the western boxers do), different kinds of kicks (high, low, roundhouse kicks and so on), elbows, clinching, knees and sweeps. I warmly recommend to try out thaiboxing if you would like to improve your balance, health, muscles or just for having some fun. As an amateur, thaiboxing is very enjoyable especially when you have the same kind of mindset as your training partners. One can always find new challenges among the sport, like competing in competitions. Self-improvement never stops.
